# Birdland translation glossary — the lock table

Every translation on this site, human or AI, follows this table. Rule of thumb:
**terms of trade and terms of art stay in English**; brand voice translates.

## Never translated (keep exactly as written)

Product & brand names
: Birdland · ABrief · AsiaSource · CostNow · Team Desk · Terminal

Materials & grades
: SK5 · 65Mn · 420J2 · S50C · 40Cr · 304 / SUS304 · 6061 · SPCC · PA6-GF30 ·
  PA66 · POM · TPR · TPE · EVA · PP · PVC · rPET · LDPE · EPDM · NBR ·
  Dacromet · HRC (hardness values, e.g. "HRC 56–60")

Trade & cost terms
: OEM · FOB · CIF · FEU · TEU · HS (code) · MFN · MPF · HMF · VAT · GST ·
  ex-works · landed cost*, Section 301

Regulation & certification marks
: EUDR · CBAM · REACH · FSC · BSCI · PEFC · ISO (with number) · CE · GS ·
  ISPM 15 · EPR · WCI (Drewry World Container Index) · PMI

Market shorthand
: USD/TWD · DXY · Brent · US 10Y · S&P 500 · STOXX 50 · VIX · HRC steel
  ("Steel HRC" as an index name)

\* "landed cost" may be glossed once per page on first use — e.g. de:
„Landed Cost (Gesamtkosten nach Ankunft)" — then used in English.

## Voice

- de: Sie-form throughout; sober trade German, no marketing superlatives.
- zh-Hant: 台灣用語(「品質」不是「质量」、「專案」不是「项目」);
  B2B 語氣,簡練不吆喝。
- ja (when it ships): です・ます商務体, no カタカナ英語 where a JIS term exists.
- All languages: sentences may be re-shaped freely; numbers, units and locked
  terms may not.

## The authoritative-English rule

Every translated page carries a footer line stating it is translated from the
English original and that the English edition is authoritative for
specifications and compliance content.
