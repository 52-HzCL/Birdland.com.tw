Privacy & device data

Birdland’s public website is designed for product, manufacturing and supply-planning information. It is not a customer project system.

What this site does not store

We do not ask this public site to store your drawings, specifications, quotations, order data, prices, email address or account credentials. Keep confidential commercial discussion in your normal email channel with Birdland.

Local preferences

Some pages may save a public region preference, report watch category, language choice, saved public report or last-visit state in your browser. These are stored only on your device and can be removed with the page’s Clear this device control or your browser’s site-data controls.

Third-party services

Opening a public OneDrive or SharePoint PDF sends you to Microsoft. Public market and news links may send you to their named publishers. Use those services only if their privacy terms suit you. Reading this site in another language sends nothing to anyone: every translated page is served from birdland.com.tw, and ABrief, AsiaSource and CostNow translate their own labels in your browser.

Calendar feeds

Birdland calendar feeds contain public planning reminders only. Your calendar application controls how and when it refreshes a subscribed feed.

Contact

For privacy questions or a request about information you have sent to Birdland by email, contact your usual Birdland representative.
