IV

One category, fifty-two years deep.

We make garden and field hand tools — trowels, pruners, shears, loppers, hoes, forks, spades — forged and formed metal first, and nothing else. No power tools, no seasonal detours into whatever is selling this year. A factory that claims every category is a trading company with a website; this one claims one line and documents it, option by option.
