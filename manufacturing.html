How long a programme actually takes.

  1. 01Specification & quotetypical 5–10 working days

    Your drawings or a reference sample, reviewed against the matrix above. You get a route, a tooling answer and a quote — and if specification OEM on existing tooling reaches what you want, we say so before quoting a tool you did not need.

  2. 02Sampling / first articletypical 15–30 days

    On existing tooling, sampling runs in weeks. The first article closes the engineering gate: the specification stops moving here.

  3. 03Tooling — only if newtypical 45–70 days

    New dies or moulds are cut only on a design-OEM programme; a specification-OEM programme on our amortised tooling skips this stage entirely. Cutting and proving the tool closes the tooling gate.

  4. 04Pilot runtypical 7–20 days

    An approval quantity through the full line and the real pack — the run that proves the fifty-thousandth piece will match the first.

  5. 05Mass productiontypical 30–60 days per order

    Order-size dependent. Outgoing inspection closes the third gate before anything is containerised.

Ranges are typical, not contractual — each stage is confirmed in writing at quotation for your programme, volume and season.
